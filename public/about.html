About PERIAK

Learn. Practise. Excel.

PERIAK is an independent digital learning platform designed to support nursing students in their academic journey, examination preparation and development of clinical knowledge.

Our mission

Our mission is to make structured, accessible and affordable nursing education resources available to students at certificate, diploma and degree levels.

What PERIAK offers

  • Structured nursing study and revision notes.
  • Past examination papers and practice examinations.
  • Interactive revision questions and mock assessments.
  • Clinical learning exercises and patient simulations.
  • Personal learning progress and revision tools.
  • Study planning and academic organization.

Our educational approach

PERIAK emphasizes understanding, active recall, examination preparation and the application of nursing knowledge to educational clinical scenarios.

Resources are organized to help learners identify important concepts, assess their understanding and improve areas requiring further revision.

Accessible learning

Students can create a free account and access available free learning resources. Optional Premium membership provides access to additional learning tools and resources.

Academic independence

PERIAK is an independent learning resource. It is not a university, nursing regulatory authority, examination board or accredited qualification provider. No institutional affiliation is implied unless explicitly stated and authorized.

Academic quality and safety

Learning materials may be prepared using human-authored and technology-assisted methods. Errors or outdated information may occur.

Students should verify clinical information against their curriculum, recognized textbooks, current clinical guidelines and qualified educators.

PERIAK does not provide patient-specific medical advice and must not replace professional clinical judgment.

Help us improve

We welcome constructive feedback, academic corrections and suggestions from nursing students and educators.
