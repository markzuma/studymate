Request account deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your PERIAK account and associated personal information.

How to submit your request

  1. Email periaksupport@gmail.com or select the button below.
  2. Provide your registered email or username.
  3. Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  4. Complete account ownership verification.

Request account deletion

Signed-in users can also submit requests through Help and Feedback.

What happens next?

Requests are manually processed following account ownership verification. Account deletion may permanently remove learning progress, bookmarks, exam history, referral benefits and Premium access.

Limited information may be retained where legally necessary for financial, security or fraud-prevention purposes. Account deletion is not an automatic refund.

Never send your password or payment PIN by email.
