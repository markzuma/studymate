Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

PERIAK is a nursing education and revision platform. This notice explains how information is processed when using our website and Android application.

Information processed

Account names, usernames, email addresses, optional profile information, study progress, bookmarks, examination responses and results, support messages, subscription and payment verification records, referral activity and application usage information.

How information is used

We use information for authentication, learning services, progress synchronization, revision recommendations, subscription management, referral rewards, support and platform security.

Service providers

PERIAK uses Supabase for authentication and database services, Vercel for hosting and GitHub for Android release distribution. Payment services may process information necessary for payment verification. These providers may also process technical and network information.

Device storage

Browser storage supports account sessions, preferences, learning progress and offline notes. Offline notes are not encrypted by PERIAK. Successful explicit logout removes the account's offline library from that browser.

Security

PERIAK uses HTTPS, authenticated access and database access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information. Limited records may need to be retained for lawful financial, security, fraud prevention or dispute-resolution purposes. Backup copies may take additional time to expire.

See our account deletion request page.

Your privacy rights and requests

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to raise concerns about its processing.

Educational use

PERIAK provides educational resources and simulated clinical learning exercises. Do not submit identifiable patient information or confidential clinical records.

Contact
